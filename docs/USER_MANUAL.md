# User Manual: IndusStock AI

## 0. Signing in
- **First start:** create the administrator account, then **save the recovery code** that is shown once.
- **Everyone else:** use the username and temporary password from the administrator; you will choose a new password.
- Use the eye icon in the password box to show or hide what you type.
- After 5 wrong passwords an account locks for 5 minutes. You are signed out after 30 idle minutes (and if you refresh the page).

### I forgot my password or username
On the sign-in page click **Forgot password or username?**
1. **E-mail code:** enter your username; a 6-digit code is e-mailed (valid 15 minutes). Enter it with a new password.
2. **Recovery code:** enter username, your saved code and a new password. You get a replacement code: save it.
3. **Ask administrator:** type your username or full name; the administrator gives you a temporary password.
4. **Forgot username:** have it e-mailed to your registered address, or ask the administrator.

Tip: open **👤 My Account** and add your e-mail and create a recovery code, so you can always recover your account.

## 1. Dashboard
Greeting, KPI cards (records, units, low, out of stock, issued in 30 days), stock-health ring, category and consumption
bars, items needing attention, the latest activity and a 30-day movement chart.

## 2. Add Spare Parts
- **Single part:** Part Name and Category are required. Set the **Minimum Stock Level** to get alerts. Part numbers must be unique.
- **Bulk import:** download the template, fill it, upload it. Check the preview (✅ OK / ⚠️ Skip with the reason) and confirm.

## 3. View Inventory
Search by any words, filter by category and status, export CSV / Excel. Open **🔍 Part details and history** for one part.
Administrators also get **Edit / Archive** and **Archived Parts** (restore).

## 4. Stock In / Stock Out
Pick the part, choose Stock In or Stock Out, enter quantity and a reference (work order, PO, machine). Stock Out above the available quantity is blocked.

## 5. Low Stock Alerts
Priority list with stock meters and suggested reorder quantities (restores stock to twice the minimum). Export it for a requisition.

## 6. Transaction History
Filter by date, part and type; totals, chart and full list; export.

## 7. Stock Analytics
Trends, most-consumed parts, stock by category, **days-of-cover forecast** (Critical ≤ 15 days, Watch ≤ 45 days) and idle stock.

## 8. AI Assistant (ElectroBot)
Ask in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, by typing or speaking. Stock questions are answered exactly from the database; engineering
questions go to Gemini. ElectroBot never changes stock; a qualified engineer must verify replacement advice.

## 9. Administrator pages
- **Reports & Backup:** Excel management report, database backup, audit log, system status, Gemini and e-mail tests.
- **User Management:** all users; add user (secure temporary password is generated); edit, deactivate, reset password, unlock,
  issue a recovery code; **Reset Requests** from the sign-in page; **Sign-in Activity**.
  You cannot deactivate yourself, and the last active administrator cannot be demoted.

## 10. My Account
Profile, e-mail, change password, create a new recovery code, and your recent account activity.
